FULL DESCRIPTION
GrowUrDhan is a family vault, not a brokerage.

Most money apps want your mobile number so someone can sell you a SIP. This one does not. You create a vault on the phone. Email is only a lock. Statements never go to our servers — we do not have servers for your books.

What you can do
• Import monthly CAS from CDSL, NSDL, CAMS, KFin, and NPS
• Add PPF, EPF, gold, property, unlisted, and loans
• See Self, spouse, children, and HUF together
• Keep a locked backup only you can open

Price (Google Play only)
• 30-day free trial
• Lifetime licence ₹249 — once, tied to your Play account, works on a new phone. Does not include support.
• Optional annual support ₹299 / year — only after you hold the lifetime licence
There is no card form inside the app. Cancel support any time; the lifetime licence stays.

Not for children. Not an adviser. A tracker for people who already own Indian assets.
