Privacy policy

GrowUrDhan is a local-first family wealth tracker for India. Your portfolio, PAN, and statements stay on the phone you installed. We do not run a cloud login, we do not serve ads, and we do not sell data.

What we collect

Nothing on our servers. The vault you create (name, email used as a username, PAN you type, date of birth, security-question answers as hashes, holdings, and imported CAS text) is stored only in an encrypted file on the device.

Google Play, if you buy a licence, processes the purchase. Google may see your Play account, device, and payment method. That is Google’s processing, not ours. We never see your card.

What we do not collect

  • Mobile number
  • Advertising ID for ads (there are no ads)
  • Contacts, location, photos, or SMS
  • Analytics or crash dumps that include holdings

Encryption and other apps

The vault is encrypted with AES-256. On Android the wrapping key lives in the hardware-backed Android Keystore. Other apps, remote-access tools, and a copied APK cannot read the file without that key and your vault password. If you switch on scheduled backup, encrypted .kosha files are written only to a folder you pick (Google Drive, OneDrive, Downloads, or another document tree). We do not operate those clouds and cannot open the files.

Account deletion

Family → Delete account erases the vault on that phone. Because we hold no cloud copy, deletion on the device is deletion everywhere we can reach. Instructions: delete-account.

Children

GrowUrDhan is for adults who manage family money. It is not directed at children under 13, and it is not a Designed for Families app.

Contact

Privacy questions: use the support email listed on the Play Store listing. We cannot recover a vault if you forget the password — that is the privacy promise.
