Delete your vault

GrowUrDhan does not keep a copy of your books on a server. Deleting the vault on the phone removes everything we stored.

  1. Open GrowUrDhan.
  2. Unlock with your vault email and password.
  3. Go to Family.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

That wipes holdings, statements, and the login on that device. A locked .kosha backup is the only way to restore.

Your Google Play licence is separate. It stays on the Play account so you can install again later. To stop a support subscription, use Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

If you cannot open the app, uninstalling GrowUrDhan also deletes the on-device vault (Android does not back it up).
